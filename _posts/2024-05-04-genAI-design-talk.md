# Using natural language to design complex systems

This post presents a demo of using natural language and generative AI to design a complex system. You’ll see a process that moves beyond traditional wireframing tools by leveraging diagramming languages to represent the different possible experiences of the same product.

The demonstration will show how these diagrams translate into multiple types of user interface, cater for many product states, and offer a more comprehensive and dynamic approach to design compared to starting with wireframes in graphic design packages. 

In addition to discussing the theoretical benefits, you’ll get a firsthand look at the practical application of this technique for designing with natural language. While it’s an emerging technique, the aim of this is to introduce you to an alternative method that has the potential to expand the boundaries of user experience design.​​
